Operation Summer Heat: the FBI says its 2026 summer surge against violent crime made 8,927 arrests, recovered 5,013 illegally possessed weapons and identified or located 1,410 child victims — after 8,629 arrests in the first Summer Heat in 2025. What do those numbers really prove?

This case file follows Operation Summer Heat in raw FBI footage: a Louisiana State Police helicopter chase in New Orleans, FBI Baltimore's pre-dawn fugitive surge (109 arrests), FBI Columbia's seizures in South Carolina, the DEA and FBI's 662 arrests in Louisiana, a Rhode Island fugitive caught after 20+ years — and what a former FBI agent calls "a very misleading statistic."
