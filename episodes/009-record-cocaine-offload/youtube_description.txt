The biggest drug bust the U.S. Coast Guard ever unloaded: 76,140 pounds of cocaine and marijuana, worth an estimated $473 million, offloaded from the cutter Hamilton at Port Everglades, Florida, on Aug. 25, 2025. How 19 cocaine boats were caught at sea, how Coast Guard helicopters and boarding teams stop go-fast smugglers, and why 2025 became the Coast Guard's record cocaine year — nearly 510,000 pounds seized.

This case file follows the record offload, the cutter Stone's single-patrol record, the Munro's 22,052-pound seizure from one boat, and what happens next in federal court.
