He Called Himself the Dead End Boss Man — Now It's 25 Years. Kendrick "Peanut" Young, Four Deuce Oakland Crips, Dallas: ATF Operation Blue Laces, the Dead End drug market on Casey Street, the Malcolm X Boulevard house and the 300-month federal sentence.

Kendrick Jamal Young, 39, pleaded guilty in September 2025 and was sentenced on November 23, 2026 to 300 months (25 years) in federal prison. The story is told only from public court-record and agency facts: the public record does not say where Young grew up, how he joined the gang or what he did before 2024, and this video does not guess.
