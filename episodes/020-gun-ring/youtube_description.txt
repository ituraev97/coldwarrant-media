A brother from Dallas, his sister and her husband were sentenced to a combined 39.5 years in federal prison in Del Rio, Texas, for their roles in a gun-smuggling ring that, the U.S. Attorney says, had ten conspirators and moved more than 200 weapons into Mexico. ATF says the 24 guns, 4,000+ rounds and body armor found in one San Antonio house were bound for a Mexican drug cartel. #truecrime #atf #guntrafficking

The full case from the Justice Department's own record: the sister's pistol purchases and the Laredo crossings within days, the brother's profile pictures and WhatsApp messages about a "buddy" who could get requested guns, the courier stopped at the Eagle Pass port of entry with 8 handguns and 19 magazines ($150–$200 per gun, 10–12 earlier trips, by his own account), the July 16, 2025 search in San Antonio, and the sentences: 180, 150 and 144 months. Plus the "iron river" in numbers (GAO: 70% of traced guns recovered in Mexico were U.S.-sourced), the 2022 law that made gun trafficking a federal crime, two more Del Rio cases (Bobby Galvan's 24 AK-style rifles to Toluca; a North Carolina father and two sons tied to the Sinaloa Cartel by prosecutors), and the Supreme Court's 2025 ruling on Mexico's lawsuit against U.S. gun makers.
