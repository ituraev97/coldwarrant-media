## Baja Metro (2019–2020)
- Entrance found by Mexican law enforcement late Aug 2019, small industrial building in Tijuana industrial area ~½ mile west of Otay Mesa POE; 4,309 ft total, 4,068 ft north of border ("over three-quarters of a mile"); ~5.5 ft tall × 2 ft wide; average depth 70 ft; rail/cart, forced-air ventilation, high-voltage cables, entrance elevator, complex drainage; offshoot ~3,529 ft into U.S. that ends below ground; main tunnel continues ~one city block to suspected former exit blocked by several hundred sandbags; no arrests or seizures; previous record 2,966 ft (San Diego, 2014); task force DEA/USBP/HSI/USAO; Callery quote → https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2020/01/29/longest-cross-border-tunnel-discovered-san-diego
- "Longest … ever found on the U.S. southwest border" (AP) → https://www.kpbs.org/news/2020/jan/29/authorities-find-longest-southwest-border-smugglin
- Name "Baja Metro Tunnel", Tunnel Entry Team, low-oxygen alarm → DVIDS (CBP) videos 736336 / 736339: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/736336 , https://www.dvidshub.net/video/736339
- No longer tunnel reported through Oct 2026 (checked: June 2025 2,918 ft incomplete; June 2026 1,933 ft) → https://www.wcax.com/2025/06/19/border-patrol-agents-discover-tunnel-linking-tijuana-united-states ; DOJ June 2026 release below

## Douglas / Agua Prieta (1990)
- May 1990; house in Agua Prieta → warehouse in Douglas; ~200 ft into U.S.; ~35 ft shaft; concrete-lined, electricity, air compressor, drains, pulley lift; pool table + water spigot hydraulics (~8 ft); false drain exit; architect Felipe de Jesús Corona-Verbera, linked to Guzmán organization, arrested 2003/extradited, convicted March 2006 in Tucson, 18 years, Ninth Circuit affirmed Dec 2007 → https://www.lawfuel.com/blog/drug-tunnel-architect-loses-appeal-us-attorney (USAO D. Ariz. release reprint)
- Depth reported as 30 ft by Arizona Daily Star (shown on screen as alternative) → https://tucson.com/news/local/border/article_997927eb-3aa1-5459-8266-68c58907224c.html

## San Diego Tunnel Task Force & 2010–2011 tunnels
- Formed 2003; ICE HSI, CBP/Border Patrol, DEA, California Bureau of Narcotic Enforcement; electronic surveillance, detective work, public tips; Nov 2011: >14 tons (Nov 15), 612 yards, rail cars, lighting, reinforced walls, hydraulic steel door + elevator in Mexico, >32 tons marijuana, six arrested → https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2011/11/30/second-major-cross-border-drug-tunnel-discovered-south-san-diego-month
- Nov 3, 2010: 20 tons seized by ICE, 4.5 tons by Mexican soldiers → https://www.kpbs.org/news/border-immigration/2010/11/03/border-tunnel-discovered
- Nov 25, 2010 (Thanksgiving): nearly half a mile, ~90 ft deep, two U.S. exits ~800 ft apart, shaft under kitchen floor in Tijuana, rail/electric/ventilation/lights, >20 tons, 8 arrests (5 in Mexico) → https://www.kpbs.org/news/2010/nov/26/federal-officials-find-another-drug-smuggling-tunn
- Sánchez Villalobos: "high-ranking operative for Mexico's Sinaloa drug cartel," indicted over 2010 & 2011 tunnels → https://ice.gov/news/releases/high-ranking-sinaloa-drug-cartel-member-indicted-tunnel-probe ; extradited Jan 2020, "Lord of the Tunnels" → https://digital-release.borderreport.com/hot-topics/border-crime/lord-of-the-tunnels-extradited-from-mexico-to-california-on-federal-drug-charges/amp/ ; pleaded guilty, sentenced July 28, 2021 to 10 years 1 month → https://www.latinpost.com/articles/151315/20210729/el-chapo-sinaloa-cartel-narco-tunnels-builder-gets-10-years.htm , https://chronline.com/stories/architect-for-el-chapos-cross-border-narco-tunnels-sentenced-to-10-years,269874
