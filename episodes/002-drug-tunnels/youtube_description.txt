In 2019, Mexican law enforcement found the entrance to a tunnel in a Tijuana industrial area. It ran about 70 feet underground into Otay Mesa, California — 4,309 feet long, with rails, ventilation, high-voltage power and an elevator. The longest smuggling tunnel ever found along the U.S. southwest border. This case file traces the tunnels under the U.S.–Mexico border: from a hydraulic floor under a pool table in 1990, to warehouse-to-warehouse "super tunnels," to the federal task force that hunts them.
