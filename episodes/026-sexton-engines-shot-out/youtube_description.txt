On Oct. 28, 2026, a 154-foot Coast Guard cutter says it stopped two suspected drug boats with disabling fire, 175 miles off Cartagena, Colombia: about 10,900 pounds of cocaine, an estimated $89.2 million, ten people detained. This is what the public record supports about what disabling fire is, how a Key West fast response cutter ended up doing it, and what happened seven days later. Coast Guard, disabling fire, cocaine seizure, Caribbean, fast response cutter.

This is a November 2026 story. No video of the firing was released; the aerial video and photos show the boats and cargo after they were stopped. The Coast Guard has not said who fired or with what, and the people detained are suspects, not convicted of anything. No individual is named in this video.
