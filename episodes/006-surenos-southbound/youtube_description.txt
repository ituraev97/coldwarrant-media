In the spring of 2014, federal agents arrested 638 gang members and associates from 145 gangs in 179 U.S. cities — most of them Sureños, the "southerners" of Southern California. This case file follows the network behind the number 13: the Mexican Mafia prison gang it pays tribute to, the "rent" Florencia 13 collected in South L.A., the 2007 Joker's Wild takedown, ICE's Project Southbound and Project Shadowfire, and the racketeering cases that reached all the way to 2026.
