# CASE FILE 006 — sources (claim → page opened)

## Cold open / IV. Project Southbound (2014)
- March 12 – April 13, 2014; month-long HSI operation targeting gangs affiliated with the Sureños; 150 federal, state and local partners; 638 gang members and associates from 145 gangs in 179 U.S. cities + 119 others = 757 arrests; >73% Sureño members or associates; 525 criminal charges, 113 administrative immigration arrests; 414 violent criminal histories, 7 wanted for murder, 5 for rape/sexual assault; 256 foreign nationals from 21 countries; 9 MS-13 members arrested in Maryland on RICO charges; San Francisco father and son: state narcotics and firearms charges, 4,669 marijuana plants, 25 lbs processed marijuana, AR-15, stolen handgun, four generators, four vehicles, $85,635; seizures: 54 firearms, drugs, >$166,000; Winkowski (Principal Deputy Assistant Secretary): "largest-ever ICE operation targeting the Sureños gang", >30,000 U.S. members and growing; Sureños pay tribute to the Mexican Mafia, "13" = M; 2011 NGIC assessment: growing faster than other national-level gangs; Operation Community Shield since Feb. 2005: >33,000 gang-related arrests, ~2,600 gangs, >438 gang leaders → https://ice.gov/news/releases/hsi-arrests-638-gang-members-during-month-long-operation (Apr. 30, 2014)
- Claude Arnold (ICE HSI SAC Los Angeles): Sureños "an umbrella group here in Los Angeles", Spanish for "southerners", affiliate with the Mexican Mafia; 132 gang members taken off the streets in Los Angeles; quote "Gang members tend to be one-man crime waves, so an individual gang member perpetrates a lot of crime" → https://abc7.com/post/surenos-gang-targeted-in-project-southbound/41582/ (May 1, 2014)
- DVIDS footage descriptions (Southbound: >600 gang members, 145 gangs, 179 cities) → https://www.dvidshub.net/video/333985 · /334111 · /333982 · /334114 · /333976
- "Cliques": "hundreds of cliques across the U.S." → ICE release above

## I. The number thirteen
- Mexican Mafia formed in 1957 at Deuel Vocational Institution (Tracy, Calif.); gangs add "13" or "X13" to show loyalty; 13 = M, 13th letter → Police Magazine, Sgt. (Ret.) Richard Valdemar, "History of the Mexican Mafia prison gang" (July 25, 2007): https://www.policemag.com/372989/history-of-the-mexican-mafia-prison-gang
- Mexican Mafia "one of the largest and most violent prison gangs in California"; Sureños = south-state Hispanic street gang aligned with the Mexican Mafia (EME); Norteños = north-state gang aligned with Nuestra Familia; Norteños and Sureños "comprise the largest number of street gang members in California" → California Attorney General, Organized Crime in California 2009: https://www.oag.ca.gov/sites/all/files/agweb/pdfs/publications/org_crime2009.pdf
- La Eme = the letter M → ICE release above (13 stands for M) · Britannica "Mexican Mafia": https://www.britannica.com/topic/Mexican-Mafia

## II. The rent — Florencia 13 / Operation Joker's Wild (2007–2009)
- Oct. 30, 2007: second wave of arrests; four indictments returned by federal grand jury (Sept. 27); 102 defendants; after first phase two weeks earlier 80 in custody; F13 controlled drug distribution in unincorporated areas south of L.A. and areas such as Huntington Park; "drug distribution dens" in South L.A.; involved in shootings of African-Americans in areas it claimed; allegedly controlled by an incarcerated Mexican Mafia member and Mexican Mafia members on the street; leaders collected "rent" from gang members and others in exchange for Mexican Mafia protection in prison or jail; 3-year investigation by LA HIDTA Task Force (LASD, DEA, ATF, ICE, LAPD, IRS-CI, FBI, LA County Probation); U.S. Attorney Thomas P. O'Brien: >100 linked to F13 face federal charges and up to life; Sheriff Lee Baca quote "Gangs be warned – we are coming for you and you will find no rest or security from law enforcement." → https://www.dea.gov/sites/default/files/divisions/la/2007/la103007p.html
- Jan. 13, 2009: ten F13 members and associates convicted after 3½-month trial (RICO, RICO conspiracy, some RICO conspiracy to murder, drug counts); 76 convicted overall; 96 of 102 in custody; "largest gang takedown in American history" → https://www.dea.gov/sites/default/files/divisions/la/2009/la011309p.html

## III. The spread
- NGIC 2011 National Gang Threat Assessment: sureño gangs "expanding faster than other national-level gangs, both in membership and geographically"; 20 states + D.C. report increase of sureño migration; migration into northern California and neighboring states (Arizona, Nevada, Oregon); sureños in California and South Carolina associated with Los Zetas (2010 NGIC reporting); sureños listed among gangs with ties to Mexican DTOs, gangs involved in human trafficking, gangs with members who served in the U.S. military; ~1.4 million gang members, >33,000 gangs, 40% increase from ~1 million in 2009; estimates not exact counts → https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/stats-services-publications-2011-national-gang-threat-assessment-2011%20national%20gang%20threat%20assessment%20%20emerging%20trends.pdf
- Note: the quote card paraphrases the NGIC sentence with "Sureños" as subject (the report text: "sureño gangs, including MS-13, 18th Street, and Florencia 13, are expanding faster than other national-level gangs, both in membership and geographically").
- "SUR" graffiti shown by an HSI agent in Conroe, Texas → DVIDS 456021 (Project Shadowfire B-roll, Texas) https://www.dvidshub.net/video/456021

## V. Project Shadowfire (2016)
- Feb. 15 – March 21 (2016), five-week HSI surge under Operation Community Shield; 1,133 arrests, 915 gang members and associates; 1,001 criminal charges, 132 administrative; most U.S. citizens; 239 foreign nationals from 13 countries; most affiliated with MS-13, Sureños, Norteños, Bloods and several prison-based gangs; drug trafficking, human smuggling and sex trafficking, murder, racketeering; most activity: Los Angeles, San Juan, Atlanta, San Francisco, Houston, El Paso; 150 firearms, >20 kg narcotics, >$70,000; Community Shield >40,000 gang-related arrests, >8,000 firearms; earlier operations Southern Tempest (2011), Project Nefarious (2012), Project Southbound (2014), Project Wildfire (2015) → https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/ice-arrests-more-1100-operation-targeting-gangs
- DVIDS Shadowfire B-roll (dated March 28, 2016) → https://www.dvidshub.net/video/456021 · /456019 · /456016 · /456015

## VI. Orders from a cell
- F13 2013 RICO case: August 2013 indictment naming 30 members/associates; 25 convicted; Giselle Casado ("secretary" to the gang's "shot-caller"; relayed messages, controlled illicit money, assisted drug trafficking) — 121 months; Jose Sanchez ("soldier"; armed security for narcotics sales at "casitas" = gang-run illegal gambling houses) — 238 months; judge: "consistently led a life of crime"; four-week trial ended July 2016; drug sales on street and smuggled into L.A. County jails; ATF, LASD, CDCR, Huntington Park PD, LAPD → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/two-convicted-racketeering-offenses-related-criminal-activities-florencia-13-street (May 9, 2017)
- Jose Dorado and Tannous Fazah: life without parole; Judge Beverly Reid O'Connell; March 2010, incarcerated senior F13 member ordered them to beat and kill a junior member; within hours victim attacked in an alley in Huntington Park and later fatally shot; prosecutors: "[T]here is no crime more serious than murder" → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/two-florencia-13-gang-members-convicted-racketeering-acts-including-murder-sentenced (June 8, 2017)
- Pomona case: Michael Lerma, 69, "Pomona Mike", full member of the Mexican Mafia; Feb. 2012 – June 2020 controlled and extorted drug proceeds from Latino street gangs in and around Pomona and from incarcerated Latinos at Calipatria State Prison; June 2020 directed three Pomona gang members to kill "S.B." at MDC Los Angeles over unpaid drug debts; jury verdict March 2025; four defendants sentenced to life by Judge George H. Wu; nine convictions in the case; prosecutors: "his cruelty, his cunning, and his power"; FBI San Gabriel Valley Safe Streets Task Force (FBI, LASD, Pomona PD, El Monte PD, DEA) → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/four-pomona-gang-members-and-mexican-mafia-associates-sentenced-life-federal-prison (March 12, 2026)
