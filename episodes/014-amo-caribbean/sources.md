## May 13–14, 2026 — Black Hawk / 391 lb north of San Juan (cold open, ch. I, close)
- May 13, 2026 AMO aircrew detected a Target of Interest: 25-ft blue yola-type vessel, three people, visible packages, northwest of Puerto Rico; Caribbean Air and Marine Branch and CAMOC maintained surveillance as it approached PR; May 14 San Juan Marine Unit deployed two Coastal Interceptor Vessels guided by aircrew vectors, CAMB UH-60 helicopter crew assisting; intercepted north of San Juan; suspects attempted to jettison contraband, agents recovered the bales and electronic devices; three Dominican Republic nationals in custody; five bales, 178 kg cocaine → CBP description of DVIDS video 1008245: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1008245
- Tracked overnight; boat tried to flee when federal units approached; those aboard threw packages into the water; CBP Black Hawk crew used air-disabling fire to stop the boat north of San Juan; ~391 lb; handed to Homeland Security Task Force investigators; CBP says the case reflects a broader Caribbean route feeding drugs toward the U.S., South Florida a major entry/distribution hub; on the same Friday (day of CBP statement) ~900 lb seized off Miami in a separate case → CBS12/Sinclair (Grace Bellinghausen, May 31, 2026): https://fox11online.com/news/nation-world/aerial-fire-ends-nearly-400-pound-cocaine-smuggling-attempt-near-puerto-rico
- Infrared footage shows men throwing items overboard; search found empty plastic containers and other unidentified packages; Christopher Hunter, Caribbean Air and Marine Branch Director: "Our Air and Marine Operations teams demonstrated exceptional skill and coordination in this interdiction."; credited the Black Hawk crew's "decisive use of air disabling fire" with stopping the vessel → Fox News (May 29, 2026, quoting CBP statement): https://www.foxnews.com/politics/watch-black-hawk-assists-takedown-cocaine-haul-puerto-rico
- No defendant is named in the script. (A USAO-PR roundup lists a May 28, 2026 nine-count indictment of three Dominican nationals over ~178 kg from a vessel — not used, because the release does not tie it to this interdiction: https://www.justice.gov/usao-pr/pr/indictments-convictions-through-guilty-pleas-and-sentencings-homeland-security-task-2)

## Ch. II — Puerto Rico as a "bridge"
- Reggie Johnson, Chief Patrol Agent, Ramey Sector: PR a "bridge to the U.S.", closer to Venezuela, Colombia, DR than to the mainland; most drugs arrive by boat; flights from the Caribbean into the U.S. are domestic; "The Caribbean has always been a hot spot for cocaine."; Border Patrol cocaine FY2023 5,300 lb, FY2024 5,900 lb, FY2025 so far 9,600 lb (article Aug. 23, 2025) → The Daily Signal: https://www.dailysignal.com/2025/08/23/fewer-people-more-cocaine-smuggling-operations-through-puerto-rico/
- Ramey Sector covers Puerto Rico (and the U.S. Virgin Islands) → same article (Ramey Sector, PR).
- Sept. 22, 2026: 17 indicted (grand jury Sept. 1, 2026, D.P.R.); HSI-led with DEA and FBI; alleged route via Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport and the U.S. mail to the continental U.S.; Michael Mayer quote "Puerto Rico will not be used as a gateway for cocaine destined for communities across the United States."; 10-year mandatory minimum up to life; indictment only an allegation, presumed innocent → DEA: https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2026/09/22/dea-caribbean-division-joins-hstf-partners-disrupting-cocaine-trafficking
- Operation Path to Zero (USPIS-led, launched Jan. 2026): >556 kg cocaine, >448 USPS parcels bound for the mainland; traffickers "exploiting Puerto Rico and the Caribbean as a transit point to the mainland United States" → DEA, May 21, 2026: https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2026/05/21/dea-caribbean-division-joins-ondcp-director-hidta-and-federal-partners

## Ch. IV — April 28–29, 2026, 1,418 lb in 20 fuel containers
- Detection ~10:52 p.m. Apr. 28 by AMO Multi-Role Enforcement Aircraft; ~35 nm SE of PR; 30-ft yola, two outboards, multiple fuel containers; AMO UH-60 Black Hawk maintained surveillance; Coastal Interceptor Vessels from Ponce and Fajardo; agents fired two warning rounds, operator stopped; interception ~12:58 a.m. Apr. 29; 643.3 kg (1,418 lb) cocaine bricks in 20 black fuel containers; three Venezuelan nationals charged by complaint (possession with intent to distribute; importation); HSTF leads; U.S. Attorney W. Stephen Muldrow committed to pursuing transnational traffickers and bringing them before the courts → USAO-PR, May 4, 2026: https://www.justice.gov/usao-pr/pr/three-venezuelans-arrested-1418-pounds-cocaine-seized-marine-interdiction-southeast
- Coast Guard tactic: marksman fires warning shots on command; if the vessel keeps running, fires again aiming at the engines; CAPT Dan Broadhurst: helicopter is "the safest way to disable these vessels at high speed on the open ocean" → MyCG, May 7, 2026: https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/4461549/behind-the-scenes-with-hitron/
- Sept. 2025: AMO Marine Interdiction Agents (Michael O. Maceda Marine Unit) used disabling fire to stop a smuggling vessel off PR; 16 Dominican nationals arrested → DVIDS 987448 (published Sept. 28, 2025): https://www.dvidshub.net/video/987448
