How a Black Hawk stopped a cocaine boat off Puerto Rico: on May 14, 2026, a CBP Air and Marine Operations helicopter crew used "air disabling fire" to stop a fleeing yola north of San Juan — 391 pounds of cocaine, thrown overboard and recovered. This drug bust documentary uses CBP's own infrared and agents' video of the chase.

Why Puerto Rico is the Caribbean "bridge" for cocaine headed to the U.S. mainland, how CBP P-3s and Multi-Role Enforcement Aircraft find drug boats at sea (including a semi-submersible carrying 16,938 pounds), the 1,418 pounds hidden in 20 fuel containers, bales dumped in the water, the 2026 seizures around one island — about 3,900 pounds in four cases — and what happens next on the dock and in federal court.
