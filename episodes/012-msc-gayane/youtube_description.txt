Nearly 20 tons of cocaine — 39,525 pounds, about $1.3 billion — hidden in seven containers on one cargo ship, the MSC Gayane, seized in Philadelphia in June 2019. The biggest cocaine bust in U.S. Customs and Border Protection history: how speedboats, a ship's crane, fake seals and eight crew members smuggled it, and what happened to the ship and the sailors.

This case file follows the MSC Gayane cocaine bust in CBP's own footage of the seizure: the night pickups at sea, the "moving hiding place" inside the ship, the crew recruited in Montenegro, the X-ray truck and the brick count, the $50 million release of the ship, the eight prison sentences — and the alleged organizer's Brooklyn case, still waiting for a trial after a juror-bribery plot.
