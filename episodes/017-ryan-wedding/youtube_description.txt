Feds say ex-Olympian Ryan Wedding paid a Mexican cartel boss about $100,000 a month for police secrets — after a $500,000 "initiation fee." This Ryan Wedding documentary follows the former Olympic snowboarder from the 2002 Salt Lake City Games to the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list and his January 2026 arrest in Mexico City, then into the November 2026 indictments: "El General" of the Cártel Nuevo Imperio (killed by Mexican agents days before his U.S. indictment was unsealed), a Mexican judge arrested in Colombia over an alleged $2 million bribe, and a fugitive L.A. gang member accused of passing law-enforcement secrets.

Ryan Wedding has pleaded not guilty. He and every defendant named in this video are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court. All allegations come from U.S. indictments, Justice Department and Treasury releases, and the news reports cited below.
