40 years for one morning. The full court-records story of the Bronx gang member sentenced for killing an 18-year-old rapper in Brooklyn: the indictment, the plea, and what is still unknown.

Paul Johnson, 25, a/k/a "PJ Glizzy," pleaded guilty and was sentenced on November 20, 2026 by U.S. District Judge Victor Marrero to 40 years in prison for conspiracy to commit murder in aid of racketeering and related counts. The victim, Jayquan McKenley, 18, performed as "Chii Wvttz."
