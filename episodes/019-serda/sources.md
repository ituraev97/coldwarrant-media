  - Orlando "Lando" Muckle, 52, Athens: 360 months + 10 years supervised release (Case 3:25-CR-20); pleaded guilty (possession with intent to distribute cocaine); cocaine through sources linked to the Gulf Cartel; controlled purchases through a confidential source; surveillance: stash apartment on Jennings Mill Parkway, another on Macon Highway; July 2025 searches: 26 kg cocaine he admitted he planned to distribute.
  - Raul Perez-Mondragon, 30, Querétaro, Mexico: charged by indictment (3:26-CR-17), initial appearance Nov. 5 before Magistrate Judge Charles Weigle, detained, immigration detainer; allegedly concealed meth inside cabbages wrapped in green cellophane, ~1 kg per package, shipments up to 300 packages; Nov. 2025 search of a residence in Athens: > 100 kg meth and multiple firearms. Presumed innocent.
