## Otay Mesa, Oct. 9, 2020 (medical supplies, 3,014 lb meth)
- Date Fri. Oct. 9, ~9:45 a.m., Otay Mesa commercial facility; tractor-trailer manifested as medical supplies; secondary inspection, imaging system + canine; 1,816 packages; ~3,014 lb meth, 64 lb heroin, 29 lb fentanyl powder, ~37 lb fentanyl pills; ~$7.2M ("over $7 million"); second-largest meth bust on the SW border in CBP history; driver 47-year-old Mexican citizen, arrested, joint ICE HSI/DEA team, to face charges; Callery quote → DEA, Oct. 11, 2020: https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2020/10/11/second-largest-border-meth-bust-history-southern-border
- Irregularities in the rear of the trailer; packages mixed in with medical supplies incl. spray bottles and pipette tools; Callery quote "This massive seizure is testament of what law enforcement agencies can do when we combine forces" → 10News San Diego, Oct. 2020: https://www.10news.com/news/local-news/south-bay-news/otay-mesa-cbp-officers-make-second-largest-meth-bust-on-southern-border
- Footage of the seized packages: DVIDS 769187 (CBP, Oct. 12, 2020): https://www.dvidshub.net/video/769187

## Nogales (Mariposa), late January 2019 (cucumbers, 254 lb fentanyl)
- Seized on a Saturday (article published Thu. Jan. 31, 2019); Mariposa port; scan showed anomalies in the trailer's floor; canine alerted; 416 packages, 94 fentanyl, rest meth; 254 lb fentanyl, mostly powder with some pills; ~395 lb meth; largest fentanyl seizure in CBP history; driver Mexican, 26; complaint: hauling cucumbers Guadalajara → Rio Rico, did not own the trailer, paid ~$36, picked up the load ~10 a.m., inspected and found nothing unusual, original seal; said he didn't know, suspected a well-dressed stranger or the cucumber owners; charged with two counts of possession with intent to distribute; Juan Mariscal (HSI SAC): 1 kg fentanyl → one million fatal doses, 114 kg seized, "points to a well-organized smuggling operation"; Michael Humphries (Nogales port director): quarter-milligram ~ a few grains of salt can kill; "Most hard narcotics seized at CBP are at the ports of entry"; Arizona POE fentanyl seizures 20 lb FY2016 → 155 lb FY2018; CDC: >28,000 deaths in 2017 involved synthetic opioids other than methadone → Arizona Daily Star, Jan. 31, 2019: https://tucson.com/news/local/article_3d584107-abb9-54f8-b079-ee6e84179321.html
- Floor compartment, tractor-trailer loaded with cucumbers; dog alerted; more than twice the prior record of 118 lb found in Nebraska in 2017; 26-year-old charged with possession with intent to distribute → NBC News, Jan. 31, 2019: https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/feds-make-largest-fentanyl-bust-u-s-history-n965486
- X-ray + drug-sniffing dog at secondary; Humphries: smugglers prefer vehicles with hidden compartments → KGUN 9, Jan. 31, 2019: https://www.kgun9.com/border-watch/us-border-officials-in-arizona-make-biggest-fentanyl-seizure

## Scanners (NII)
- ~15% of arriving commercial trucks, ~98% of rail cars, ~1% of passenger vehicles examined with NII (FY2024); X-ray and gamma-ray imaging; FY2019 $570M for NII; Dec. 2024: 50 of 150 large-scale fixed systems deployed/installed, 43 in storage (June 2025 DHS OIG); P.L. 116-299 (FY2021) required a plan and cost analysis for 100% inspection of commercial and passenger vehicles → Congressional Research Service IN12601 (Sept. 2, 2025): https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/IN12601.html
- Multi-Energy Portal at the Port of Mariposa → DVIDS 907568 description: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/907568
- Low-energy portals at Bridge of the Americas, El Paso, image an occupied vehicle in motion → DVIDS 936107 description: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/936107
- CBP canines screen vehicles; trained to detect narcotics, humans, money and weapons → DVIDS 728919 description: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/728919
- CBP chemists sort, weigh and examine seized drugs → DVIDS 1002496 description: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/1002496

## Calexico, July 2026 (rebar, 1,002 lb cocaine)
- >$20M; floorboards of a commercial truck and attached flatbed; 269 bundles of rebar; X-ray anomalies; dog alerted; rebar unloaded, planks unscrewed; 366 packages, 1,002.13 lb; "a tremendous amount of drugs, even by the standards of this district" (motion to detain); second-largest cocaine seizure of 2026 in S.D. Cal.; largest in May (> a ton, ~$45M, linked to a cross-border tunnel); driver only occupant, charged with importation (21 USC 952, 960), 10-yr mandatory minimum, max life; pleaded not guilty at arraignment; presumed innocent; California Homeland Security Task Force, Operation Take Back America → DEA/USAO S.D. Cal., July 29, 2026: https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2026/07/29/estimated-20-million-worth-cocaine-seized-commercial-truck-border
- Driver age 44, entered from Mexicali, arraigned July 28; May tunnel: 1,933 ft, Tijuana → Buy 4 Less store near Otay Mesa, reportedly 55 ft deep, reinforced walls, rails, ventilation, electricity, exit under a storage-room floor concealed by a hydraulic lift, 2,269.87 lb suspected cocaine, four charged → FreightWaves, Aug. 1, 2026: https://freightwaves.com/news/20m-in-cocaine-found-beneath-floorboards-of-commercial-truck-trailer-at-california-border
