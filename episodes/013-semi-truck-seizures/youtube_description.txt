3,000 pounds of meth found in one semi truck — 3,014 pounds of methamphetamine, plus heroin and fentanyl, hidden in a trailer of "medical supplies" at the Otay Mesa border crossing in San Diego. How CBP officers find drugs hidden in semi trucks at the border: X-ray scanners, drug-sniffing dogs, and a second look.

This case file follows five real seizures at four commercial ports of entry, in CBP's own footage: the 2020 Otay Mesa meth bust and the same trick seven months later, the record 254 pounds of fentanyl under a load of cucumbers in Nogales, 1,635 pounds of meth in a pepper shipment at the Pharr bridge, and 1,002 pounds of cocaine under a flatbed's floorboards in Calexico — and the number that matters most: as of fiscal 2024, CBP scanned only about 15% of arriving commercial trucks.
