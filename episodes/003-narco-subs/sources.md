## I. Bigfoot / what an SPSS is
- Before 2006 only rumors / Colombian-jungle finds not tied to trafficking; first indisputable sighting Nov 2006 ~100 mi off Costa Rica, 3 tons cocaine, crew of four (2 Colombians, a Guatemalan, a Sri Lankan), joint CG/DEA/FBI/Colombia/Costa Rica; wood & fiberglass; ~50–100 ft; crew ~4; $500K–$1M build cost; $200–400M cargo; scuttling valves; prosecutors needed a bale as evidence; Sept 2008 seizures pushed the law (Capt. Kevin O'Day quote); Stavridis quote → https://defensemedianetwork.com/stories/twilight-of-the-semi-submersible
- Three snorkels, no diver's flag; nicknamed "Bigfoot"; grayish-blue paint, hard to see/radar; ~90 days to build; crews "plucked off a dock"; ~$3,000 per trip; ~30% of Colombia's cocaine exports (David Kushner, NPR 2009); typically scuttled after one trip → https://www.npr.org/transcripts/103490776
- "Mostly underwater, with only a cockpit and exhaust pipe visible" → https://www.dvidshub.net/news/172197/us-agencies-stop-semi-submersible-seize-12000-pounds-cocaine
