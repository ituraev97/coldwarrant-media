June 18, 2019: a Coast Guard boarding team jumps onto a moving semi-submersible in the eastern Pacific and forces open the hatch. Inside: five suspected smugglers and more than 17,000 pounds of cocaine. This case file follows the narco-subs from jungle-built "Bigfoot" in 2006 to the 2008 law that made the boats themselves a crime, the Panama Express prosecutions in Tampa, the first narco-sub in Europe, and the Coast Guard's record year.
