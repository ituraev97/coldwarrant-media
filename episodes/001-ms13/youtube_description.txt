In 2017, federal agents arrested 267 alleged members and associates of a single gang. That gang wasn't born in Central America — it was born in Los Angeles. This is the story of MS-13: how refugee kids from El Salvador's civil war built a gang in Pico-Union, how deportations exported it across Central America, and how the U.S. went from task forces to terrorism charges.
