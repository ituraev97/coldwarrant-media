A Las Vegas jury convicted Duane "Keefe D" Davis of the 1996 murder of Tupac Shakur in under three hours. Prosecutors never claimed he pulled the trigger, and the defense says nothing but his own words put him in that car. This is the story of the white Cadillac, the interviews, the memoir, the raid and the trial that ended in 40 years to life.

Convicted at trial on August 31, 2026; sentenced October 13, 2026 to 40 years to life. An appeal is expected.
