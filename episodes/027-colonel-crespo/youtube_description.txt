A former Bolivian Air Force colonel is accused of holding cocaine meetings in uniform while armed subordinates stood guard. Henry Richard Crespo-Guzman, "Colonel Crespo", was extradited to the United States on November 19, 2026. This video goes through what the Justice Department's release actually says, what it leaves out, the three counts (one with a 30-year mandatory minimum), and the earlier case of Bolivia's ex-anti-narcotics chief, Maximiliano Dávila Pérez.

He is CHARGED, NOT CONVICTED. Everything described as his conduct is an allegation, and he is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. The 30-year figure is a statutory minimum that applies only on conviction, not a prediction. The U.S. Attorney's Office has not stated how much cocaine was involved; figures in some Bolivian press are not confirmed by the release. Statements by his wife and by the Bolivian Air Force are press reports, not court findings.

The photo of the defendant is an official image from the SDNY/DEA press release. No footage in this video is from Bolivia or from this case: all aircraft and boat footage is labeled FILE FOOTAGE (U.S. government video via DVIDS, public domain).
