Washington offers up to $8 million for two brothers it says lead La Nueva Familia Michoacana — now a federal grand jury in Texas has charged them and 19 others in a RICO case tied to gold-plated guns, an engine full of cocaine and a crash that killed 8.

On Nov. 20, 2026, the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Western District of Texas announced a 21-defendant, 14-count racketeering indictment returned in Del Rio. Prosecutors name Johnny "El Pez" Hurtado Olascoaga and his brother Jose Alfredo "El Fresa" Hurtado Olascoaga as the cartel's head and No. 2. Five defendants are in custody; 16, including both brothers, are at large and believed to be in Mexico. All 21 are charged, not convicted: an indictment is merely an accusation, and every defendant is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty.
