An Aryan Brotherhood leader ran heroin and meth from his prison cell on smuggled phones — and the DEA was listening. The true story of the Aryan Brotherhood prison gang, Ronald "Renegade" Yandell, the DEA wiretaps on contraband cellphones, and the federal RICO cases that sent its leaders to life in prison. #truecrime #aryanbrotherhood #prisongang

From San Quentin around 1964 and the 2006 federal trial of Barry Mills and Tyler Bingham, to California's flood of contraband phones (10,494 confiscated in 2019 alone), the 2016 DEA wiretaps that caught Yandell bragging about directing a 2015 prison murder and Danny Troxell ordering another, the June 2019 takedown (16 charged), the nine-week trial, two consecutive life terms ("I ain't easy to kill…"), the second, ATF-investigated case out of the Central Valley (60+ arrests, 80+ lb of meth; Francis Clement convicted of five murders ordered while in state prison) — and the gunman in the 2020 Lomita double murder, convicted in August 2026 and set to be sentenced on Nov. 23 to mandatory life. Five other defendants in that case await trial and are presumed innocent.
