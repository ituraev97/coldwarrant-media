## Primary — U.S. Department of Justice / U.S. Attorney's Office, E.D. Cal.
1. OPA/USAO, Aug. 26, 2026 — "Aryan Brotherhood Associate Convicted of Two Murders": https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/aryan-brotherhood-associate-convicted-two-murders and https://www.justice.gov/usao-edca/pr/aryan-brotherhood-associate-convicted-two-murders
   - Justin Gray, 40, San Pedro; two counts murder in aid of racketeering; Oct. 4, 2020, Lomita; lured victims from San Diego; he and another AB associate shot both in the head; ordered by AB "Brother" Francis Clement at Kern Valley State Prison over "disrespect"; Baby Blue Wrecking Crew (white-only, takes orders from and works under the AB); sentencing Nov. 23 before Judge Jennifer L. Thurston, mandatory life; 21 defendants; Weaver, Pitchford, Collins, Van Beusekom, Perkins — trial Sept. 2027; ATF lead.
   - Quotes: Eric Grant "Justin Gray executed two men at point-blank range because an incarcerated gang member ordered it."; ATF Dir. Rob Cekada "These murders are a stark reminder that criminal organizations can direct violence from behind prison walls".
2. USAO E.D. Cal., Feb. 18, 2025 — Clement/Johnson/Stinson convicted: https://www.justice.gov/usao-edca/pr/three-aryan-brotherhood-prison-gang-members-convicted-rico-conspiracy-and-murder-aid
   - Four-week trial, Judge Thurston; Stinson 70 (RICO), Clement 58 (RICO + 5 VICAR murders), Johnson 63 (RICO + 2); smuggled cellphones; Stinson approved murders.
3. USAO E.D. Cal., May 20, 2025 — sentencing: https://www.justice.gov/usao-edca/pr/three-white-supremacists-sentenced-prison-racketeering-conspiracy-two-serve-life
   - Clement & Johnson life; Stinson 20 years federal; each Clement murder committed while in state prison; ordered because victims violated rules or owed money; Stinson contraband phone in cell, sponsored members, resolved disputes, approved murders; court-authorized wiretaps.
   - Quotes: Acting ATF Dir. Daniel Driscoll "These sentences send a clear message: the walls of a prison do not shield violent gang leaders from justice"; Acting USA Michele Beckwith (deadly influence over members, associates and others, inside and outside prison).
4. USAO E.D. Cal., Nov. 20, 2020 — 60+ arrested (also atf.gov): https://www.justice.gov/usao-edca/pr/more-60-aryan-brotherhood-gang-members-and-associates-arrested-violent-crime-firearms-fraud
