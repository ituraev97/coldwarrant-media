El Mencho's brother, Antonio Oseguera Cervantes — "Tony Montana" — was sentenced to 270 months (22.5 years) in U.S. federal prison on Nov. 13, 2026. This CJNG cartel documentary follows the brother of the Jalisco New Generation Cartel boss from a 1996 heroin arrest in the U.S. to meth labs for the Milenio Cartel, cartel money and precursor chemicals for El Mencho, two arrests in Mexico, the 29-defendant handover of Feb. 27, 2025, El Mencho's death in Tapalpa, and the sentencing in Washington.

Based on the Justice Department's and DEA's own releases, the guilty plea, the 2016 Treasury Kingpin Act designation, and AP and EFE reporting from the courtroom. Also in this case file: El Menchito (life + 30 years), Jessica Oseguera, José González Valencia (Los Cuinis) and the son-in-law who faked his death.
