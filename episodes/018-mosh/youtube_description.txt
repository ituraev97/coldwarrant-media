Feds offer $10 million for information leading to the conviction of a man they already arrested: Axel Bladimir Montejo Saenz, "Mosh," an alleged lieutenant of Guatemala's Los Huistas cocaine organization, arrested in San Diego — months after his father-in-law, alleged Los Huistas leader Eugenio Darío Molina-López, was arrested in the same city. This true-crime documentary follows the Justice Department's case: the 2018 indictment with a maritime "vessel" count, more than 5,500 kilograms of cocaine and $3.7 million the government links to him, Operation Guerrilla Unit's wiretaps, the 2022 Treasury sanctions, and how the family tree Treasury published came down from 2025 to 2026.

Montejo Saenz and Molina-López have pleaded not guilty. They and every person in this video who has not been convicted are presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court. All allegations come from U.S. Justice Department and Treasury releases and the news reports cited below.
