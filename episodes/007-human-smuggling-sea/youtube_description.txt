On May 2, 2021, a 40-foot boat carrying 32 passengers broke apart off Point Loma in San Diego. Three people died, and the captain was later sentenced to 18 years in federal prison. This case file follows the sea routes smugglers sell to migrants: the panga landings off Southern California, the Bimini crossing that left 34 people missing off Florida, and the Mona Passage into Puerto Rico. It also follows the Coast Guard, CBP and HSI crews and the federal prosecutors working those routes.
