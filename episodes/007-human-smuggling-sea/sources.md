# CASE FILE 007 — sources (claim → page opened)

## Cold open / II. Point Loma (May 2, 2021)
- 40-foot cabin cruiser; 32 passengers incl. 3 unaccompanied minors; agreed to pay $15,000–$18,000; captain repeatedly used controlled substances and lost consciousness at least once; boat circled for over an hour; ~6 a.m. engine failed; drifted aground in rain and large swells near Cabrillo National Monument; passengers told to stay below deck; captain jumped overboard and swam ashore; Antonio Hurtado, 40, of San Diego, pleaded guilty (3 counts attempted bringing in resulting in death, 3 counts for financial gain, 1 count assault on a federal officer — kneed a Border Patrol agent in the head); Randy Grossman: "Never trust a smuggler." → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/boat-captain-pleads-guilty-maritime-alien-smuggling-incident-resulting-three-deaths (Apr. 6, 2022)
- Sentenced to 18 years + 3 years supervised release by U.S. District Judge Janis L. Sammartino; 3 dead (Maricela Hernandez-Sanchez, Victor Perez-Degollado, Maria Eugenia Chavez-Segovia, Mexican citizens); 29 rescued; judge: "the most egregious case I've ever had in my courtroom in over 15 years in the Southern District of California"; Grossman: "Thirty-two passengers put their savings and their lives in this defendant's hands"; Chad Plantz (SAC, HSI San Diego): "Human smugglers have no respect for human life, as is demonstrated by this defendant" → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/boat-captain-fatal-maritime-alien-smuggling-incident-near-point-loma-sentenced-18-years (Aug. 12, 2022)
- Background: KPBS, May 6, 2021 → https://kpbs.org/news/2021/05/06/man-accused-piloting-boat-fatal-crash-killed-three (early reports said 4 dead; official count is 3)

## I. The pangas
- ReCoM members (CBP AMO, USBP, CBP OFO, USCG, ICE HSI, other partners); "exchange intelligence, pool assets for coverage, patrols, and response, and plan operations"; San Diego Sector since Oct. 1, 2020: 330 maritime-related events, 1,751 arrests; Aaron Heitke (Chief Patrol Agent): "Smuggling along the California coastline is inherently dangerous for all of the involved parties"; Brandon Tucker (AMO San Diego): "Smugglers don't adhere to boating safety standards." → https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/local-media-release/recom-partners-gear-labor-day-weekend (Aug. 31, 2021)
- ReCoM = DHS components + state and local agencies → https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/local-media-release/21-people-apprehended-along-san-diego-beaches-coast-after-two-boats (Jul. 3, 2020)
- Pleasure craft and "pangas" (repurposed Mexican fishing vessels); July 2021: 12 smuggling events in Orange, LA and Ventura counties → https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/maritime-smuggling-activity-increases-orange-los-angeles-and-ventura-counties (Aug. 23, 2021)
- April 2022: AMO detected three pangas, lights off, heading toward the maritime boundary, 72 Mexican nationals → DVIDS https://www.dvidshub.net/video/853936
- Aug. 24, 2017: 12 Mexican nationals, one vessel landed at Point Loma, the other at Mission Bay → DVIDS https://www.dvidshub.net/video/545682

## III. The fog
- Imperial Beach, Nov. 26, 2022: 8 aboard, 3 dead; captain Jorge Armando Preciado-Vasquez 54 months; co-captain Alexis Martinez-Preciado 50 months; passengers told to remove life vests, "a common practice so passengers are quicker and more inconspicuous" → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/captain-and-co-captain-sentenced-maritime-smuggling-effort-left-three-dead (Dec. 14, 2023)
- Black's Beach, night of March 11, 2023: two pangas, heavy fog, 8 dead; Chief James Gartland: "One of the worst maritime smuggling tragedies that I can think of in California" → https://nbcsandiego.com/news/local/8-dead-after-boat-capsizes-at-blacks-beach-sdfd/3184668
- CBP spokesperson Jason Givens: "the result of callous human smugglers, who have no regard for human life"; ReCoM death figures FY2019 3, FY2020 4, FY2021 5, FY2022 5, FY2023 13 (as of late March) → https://www.kpbs.org/news/local/2023/03/28/blacks-beach-tragedy-2023-maritime-smuggling (inewsource/KPBS, Mar. 28, 2023) · https://inewsource.org/2023/03/22/blacks-beach-tragedy-maritime-smuggling/
- No prosecutions found for Black's Beach — nobody named.
- Del Mar, May 5, 2025: 20-foot panga from Popotla, 19 aboard, capsized ~200 yards offshore; 4 dead incl. children 14 and 10; captains told passengers to remove life jackets; ~$13,500 per person; two captains pleaded guilty (Julio Cesar Zuniga Luna, Jesus Ivan Rodriguez Leyva) → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/boat-captains-plead-guilty-deadly-maritime-smuggling-events (Apr. 14, 2026)
- Three co-defendants sentenced to 16, 21 and 10 months → https://timesofsandiego.com/crime/2026/03/23/another-smuggler-gets-jail-term-deaths-of-migrants-off-del-mar/
