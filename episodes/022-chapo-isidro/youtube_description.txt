FBI Top Ten fugitive "Chapo Isidro" — Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, wanted by the U.S. since 2012 with a reward of up to $5 million — was captured by Mexican special forces in Guasave, Sinaloa on Nov. 20, 2026. Mexico's Defense Ministry says 4 soldiers were killed in the operation. The Justice Department calls him a Sinaloa Cartel senior leader; he faces narcoterrorism and material-support charges in Washington. He is charged, not convicted, and presumed innocent.

The full case: from the Beltrán Leyva "right-hand man" in the FBI's wanted notice, through the 2012 indictment, the 2013 Kingpin Act sanctions on his family and businesses in Guasave, the 2019 superseding indictment and $5 million reward, the Sinaloa Cartel's civil war after El Mayo Zambada's arrest, his Feb. 2025 spot on the FBI Ten Most Wanted list — to the Friday-night raid and the six counts he now faces.
