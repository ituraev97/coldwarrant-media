How does drug money get laundered through a U.S. bank? The TD Bank money laundering case: $18.3 trillion in transactions left unmonitored, three laundering networks moving more than $670 million, one network depositing about $474 million in cash, bank insiders paid in gift cards — and a guilty plea with about $3 billion in penalties.

This case file follows the money from bulk cash smuggling and the "$10,000 line" to David's Network, the debit cards behind more than 120,000 ATM withdrawals in Colombia, the alleged Sinaloa cartel link to Chinese underground banking (Operation Fortune Runner), and how HSI, DEA and IRS-CI follow drug money today.
