# CASE FILE 010 — sources (claim → URL)

## TD Bank plea (Oct. 10, 2024) — U.S. DOJ press release 24-1286
- TD Bank N.A. = "the 10th largest bank in the United States"; TDBNA and parent TD Bank US Holding Company pleaded guilty; TDBNA: conspiracy to fail to maintain BSA-compliant AML program, fail to file accurate CTRs, and launder money; TDBUSH: causing AML/CTR failures
- Penalties: forfeiture $452,432,302 + criminal fine $1,434,513,478.40 = $1,886,945,780.40 ("over $1.8 billion"); independent compliance monitor for three years
- Jan. 1, 2018 – Apr. 12, 2024: 92% of total transaction volume unmonitored, ~$18.3 trillion
- No new transaction monitoring scenarios and no material changes to existing ones from at least 2014 through late 2022
- More than $5 billion in transactional activity in accounts after deciding to close them
- Three networks moved more than $670 million through TD Bank accounts (2019–2023): (1) Jan. 2018 – Feb. 2021, >$470 million via large cash deposits into nominee accounts, operators gave employees gift cards >$57,000; (2) Mar. 2021 – Mar. 2023, high-risk jewelry business moved nearly $120 million through shell accounts before the bank reported it; (3) funds deposited in U.S. and withdrawn from ATMs in Colombia, five TD Bank employees conspired and issued dozens of ATM cards, ~$39 million
- Garland: "By making its services convenient for criminals, TD Bank became one"; "the first US bank in history to plead guilty to conspiracy to commit money laundering"
- Investigated by IRS-CI, FDIC-OIG, DEA; assistance from HSI, CBP, NYPD, Morristown PD, USAO-PR
→ https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/td-bank-pleads-guilty-bank-secrecy-act-and-money-laundering-conspiracy-violations-18b

## Treasury / FinCEN (Oct. 10, 2024)
- FinCEN $1.3 billion penalty; "the largest civil monetary penalty against a bank in Treasury's history" (Adeyemo); TD facilitated over $400 million in laundering for criminals selling fentanyl and other deadly drugs; gift cards as bribes; Adeyemo: "The bank enabled drug trafficking."
→ https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/files/docs/publications/treaspr/2024/2024-10-10_jy2640.pdf (Treasury press release jy2640, archived)

## Da Ying Sze ("David") — USAO D.N.J., Feb. 22, 2022
- Da Ying Sze, 43, of Queens, N.Y.; pleaded guilty Feb. 22, 2022, before Judge Esther Salas in Newark: conspiracy to commit money laundering, unlicensed money transmitting business, bribing bank employees
- 2016–2021 laundered more than $653.3 million in cash from narcotics and other illicit proceeds; fee ~1–2%
- Deposited cash at financial institutions in NY, NJ, PA and elsewhere, into accounts of shell companies and conspirators; then official bank checks, personal/business checks, domestic and international wires to thousands of people/entities in the U.S., China, Hong Kong and elsewhere
- At least $57,000 in gifts (gift cards etc.) to bank employees in 2020–2021
→ https://www.justice.gov/usao-nj/pr/queens-man-admits-orchestrating-653-million-money-laundering-conspiracy-operating

## Wilfredo Aquino — DOJ Jan. 6, 2026 (plea) and July 15, 2026 (sentence)
- TD Bank assistant store manager, New York; David's Network moved ~$474 million through TD Bank accounts via cash deposits at stores in NY, NJ and elsewhere
- ~1,680 official bank checks, >$92 million; nearly all funded by cash deposits over $10,000 that triggered CTRs; never listed David as "conductor"; gift cards from David >$11,000; Feb. 2021 three transactions totaling almost $2 million in cash into a third party's account
- A colleague warned him David's activity "looks like money laundering"
- Sentenced July 15, 2026 to 46 months in prison
