## The June 18, 2019 boarding
- Munro, Eastern Pacific, SPSS interdicted June 18; "over 17000 pounds of cocaine, worth an estimated $232 million", "largest single seizure since 2015"; 8 interdictions (5 panga-style, 1 low-profile go-fast, 1 fishing vessel, 1 SPSS); 31,000+ lb / $467M; HITRON helicopter, 230+ flight evolutions, disabling fire on outboard engines of two fleeing vessels; 98-day patrol; first counter-drug patrol since commissioning (2017); sixth Legend-class NSC; Alameda — U.S. Coast Guard Pacific Area release, July 15, 2019: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/251c1fd
- Offload July 11, 2019, San Diego (NAS North Island per CBS): 39,000+ lb cocaine + 933 lb marijuana, $569M, 14 interdictions/disruptions by three cutters May–July; Pence, Carroll, Dhillon, Fagan; Pence quotes ("fourteen operations went into this offload… largest Coast Guard removal in four years", "seizing illegal drugs at a faster rate than ever before", "Our Coast Guard deserves better"); Fagan quote; 70% of offshore presence aging medium-endurance cutters, many 50+ years — U.S. Coast Guard Pacific Area release, July 11, 2019: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/250f187 ; Sea Power copy: https://seapowermagazine.org/vice-president-participates-in-569-million-cocaine-offload-in-san-diego/

## Semi-sub background
- Defense Media Network, "Twilight of the Semi-submersible?" (Craig Collins, Nov. 21, 2010): Bigfoot Nov. 2006, 100 miles off Costa Rica, 3 tons, crew of four; two ~7-ton seizures Sept. 2008; DTVIA passed within a month (Public Law 110-407); crews scuttled boats for two years, interdictions became rescues, smugglers returned without charges; boarding at night with water over the boots; $500K–$1M build cost: https://defensemedianetwork.com/stories/twilight-of-the-semi-submersible ; "What the Semi-submersibles Mean" (2010): https://defensemedianetwork.com/stories/what-the-semisubmersibles-mean
