A U.S. Coast Guard boarding team jumps onto a narco sub that is still moving, pounds on the hatch and finds five people and almost 17,000 pounds of cocaine. This is the full story of the June 18, 2019 Munro boarding: who builds these boats, why Congress made them illegal, the $569 million San Diego offload and the sentences that followed. Narco sub, semi-submersible, Coast Guard boarding, Eastern Pacific.

This is a 2019 case, not breaking news. The three men who pleaded guilty were sentenced on Dec. 18, 2019; no one else is named in this video. Footage of the boarding and the offload is U.S. Coast Guard video released by DVIDS (public domain). All other footage is labeled FILE FOOTAGE and is not from this boarding.
