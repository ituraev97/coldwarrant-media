4 drug boats in 15 minutes: how Coast Guard snipers stop drug boats from a helicopter. On Feb. 19, 2025, south of the Galápagos, a HITRON aircrew and the cutter Stone stopped four go-fast boats — about 10,885 pounds of cocaine and eight suspected smugglers — using warning shots and disabling fire at the engines. This drug bust documentary uses the Coast Guard's own drone, infrared and boarding video.

Who HITRON is (the Coast Guard's only airborne use-of-force unit, Jacksonville, Fla.), how a marksman in an MH-65 Dolphin stops a speedboat, the 1999 first interdiction and the 1,000th in 2025, the 22,052-pound record seizure, the cutter Tahoma's "triple threat" off Colombia in 2026 — and what happens next on the dock and in federal court.
