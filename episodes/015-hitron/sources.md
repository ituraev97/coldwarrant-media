## Ch. II — what HITRON is
- Based in Jacksonville, Fla.; the Coast Guard's "only airborne use-of-force unit"; MH-65 Dolphin; mission crew two pilots + precision marksman; precision rifles and machine guns; deploys aboard cutters for weeks at a time; helicopter launches, stops the vessel, provides overwatch for the boarding team; warning shots first, then disabling fire at the engines; boarding team from the cutter moves in; own shooting program; members with little firearms experience can become instructors; Capt. Dan Broadhurst, CO: "It's fundamentally a life-saving mission"; PO1 Taylor Wolf (PM-A): "We're shooting from an aerial platform, which is very different than anywhere else."; Lt. Andy Gilliam, former Army Black Hawk pilot, Direct Commission Aviator program; established 1999; 1,000th interdiction Aug. 25, 2025 → U.S. Coast Guard MyCG, "Behind the scenes with HITRON" (May 7, 2026): https://www.mycg.uscg.mil/News/Article/4461549/behind-the-scenes-with-hitron/
- Training footage (Jacksonville, Jan. 23, 2026) → DVIDS 997828, 998143: https://www.dvidshub.net/video/997828 · https://www.dvidshub.net/video/998143

## Ch. IV — 1999 → 1,000
- First interdiction Aug. 16, 1999 by shooting out the outboard motors of a go-fast; 20th year: >500 interdictions, >$20 billion of narcotics stopped → Maritime Executive, Aug. 20, 2019: https://maritime-executive.com/article/uscg-s-sharpshooters-celebrate-20-years-and-20b-in-seizures
- 1,000th interdiction Aug. 25, 2025, Cutter Midgett, Eastern Pacific, "airborne use of force" on a non-compliant vessel, ~3,606 lb suspected cocaine (~$46M), six suspected narco-traffickers apprehended; since Oct. 1, 2024 HITRON interdicted $3.3 billion, ~3× its historic annual average of $1 billion (release dated Sept. 10, 2025) → USCG GovDelivery: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCG/bulletins/3f20109
- Munro departed Alameda Nov. 3, 2025; "warning shots and disabling fire from the HITRON aircraft"; 22,052 lb of cocaine; six detained; "the single largest maritime drug seizure in 18 years" → USCG press release: https://news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4426962/coast-guard-cutter-munro-crew-returns-home-after-119-day-multi-mission-patrol-m/ (date Dec. 2, 2025 per DVIDS 989073 and Fox News, Dec. 5, 2025: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/coast-guard-helicopter-sniper-takes-out-narco-boat-engines-stunning-exclusive-video)
