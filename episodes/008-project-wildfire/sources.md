## Cold open / III. Wildfire / IV. The count (Project Wildfire, 2015)
- Feb. 23 – Mar. 31, 2015; led by HSI National Gang Unit; 215 state, local and federal partners incl. ERO; part of Operation Community Shield; 976 gang members and associates from 239 gangs in 282 cities; 1,207 total arrests (1,057 men, 150 women); 231 others on federal/state/immigration violations; 913 criminal charges, 63 administrative; 650 violent criminal histories, 19 active murder warrants, 15 rape/sexual-assault warrants; 199 foreign nationals from 18 countries, of whom 151 gang members/associates; majority U.S. citizens; mostly Sureños, Norteños, Bloods, Crips, Puerto Rican-based gangs, prison gangs; busiest: San Juan, Dallas, El Paso, Los Angeles, Detroit; Puerto Rico/central Florida 46 members of the Zorrilla criminal organization (narcotics, money laundering, related charges); Lubbock 122 (Bloods, Crips, Mexican Mafia, Sureños, Texas Syndicate, Aryan Brotherhood); Detroit area 89 (Latin Counts, Folk Nation, Sureños…); Chicago area 30 (Latin Kings, Gangster Disciples, Vice Lords); Imperial Valley 28 incl. 10 documented Brole and Centro members; seizures 82 firearms, 5.2 kg meth, 7.8 kg marijuana, 5.6 kg cocaine, 1.5 kg heroin, $379,399, counterfeit goods $547,534 MSRP, 5 vehicles; Community Shield since Feb. 2005: 36,000+ arrests; Sarah R. Saldaña: "Criminal gangs inflict violence and fear upon our communities." → https://ice.gov/news/releases/ice-arrests-976-gang-members-and-associates-during-project-wildfire-surge (Apr. 8, 2015)
- Saldaña: such groups "can spread like a cancer" without law-enforcement attention → https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/89-detroit-area-arrests-in-national-crackdown-on-gangs/ (Apr. 8, 2015; quoting the ICE release)
- Mike Prado (acting deputy assistant director, HSI): "one of the largest operations we've ever conducted and it's the most successful operation we've conducted"; "We want to keep these gang members on their heels and constantly looking over their shoulder"; 2012 OJJDP study: 30,000+ gangs in the U.S.; experts: big busts are short-term fixes, recruitment must be addressed → https://www.csmonitor.com/USA/USA-Update/2015/0409/Project-Wildfire-bust-clears-1-000-suspected-gang-members-off-streets (Apr. 9, 2015)

## II. Razor's Edge (2013)
- July 15 – Aug. 9 (2013); HSI National Gang Unit under Operation Barbed Wire; targeted MS-13; 25 HSI field offices worked with local, state, tribal and federal agencies on threat assessments; 361 arrested; 263 gang members/associates from 43 gangs in 71 cities; 158 MS-13; 4 gang leaders; 202 foreign nationals from 15 countries; charges incl. first-degree murder, armed robbery, kidnapping, illegal re-entry, sexual assault; seized 28 firearms, 9.94 kg marijuana, 771.50 g heroin, $22,390.85; Petersburg, Va.: 19-year-old Salvadoran MS-13 member arrested for first-degree murder and illegal re-entry; New Mexico: MS-13 member + 2 associates, 5 stolen handguns, ~½ oz heroin; James Dinkins quote → https://ice.gov/news/releases/361-arrested-during-nationwide-gang-operation (Aug. 16, 2013)

## V. The record (2017 surge)
- Mar. 26 – May 6, 2017; largest HSI gang surge to date; 1,378 arrests; 1,098 criminal charges incl. 21 murder-related, 7 rape/sexual assault; 280 administrative; 933 U.S. citizens; 445 foreign nationals from 21 countries; 1,095 confirmed gang members/affiliates (Bloods 137, Sureños 118, MS-13 104, Crips 104); 283 no gang affiliation claimed; criteria incl. admitting membership, conviction under gang-related laws, identifying tattoos, named by reliable source; 10 crossed as unaccompanied minors, 9 confirmed gang members, 8 MS-13; seized 238 firearms, $491,763; Falls Church Apr. 26: 11 MS-13 members at a stash house surveilled over sex-trafficking reports; San Antonio Apr. 5: Tango Orejón associate, cocaine, meth, heroin, 4 handguns (1 stolen), $48,000+; San Diego BEST: 23 arrests, 17 firearms, ammunition; Newark: 2.8 kg raw heroin; Thomas Homan (Acting Director): "Gangs threaten the safety of our communities, not just in major metropolitan areas but in our suburbs and rural areas, too." → https://ice.gov/news/releases/ice-led-gang-surge-nets-1378-arrests-nationwide (May 11, 2017)

## VI. Matador (2017–2018)
- Began May 2017; MS-13 and other transnational gangs in Long Island, NYC, Hudson Valley; Mar. 29, 2018: 24 arrests → 475 total; 227 criminal, 248 administrative; MS-13 274, 18th Street 15; Nassau 210, Suffolk 177, Bronx 18, Queens 36, Brooklyn 12, Spring Valley 19, other states 3; partners incl. CBP, USCIS, Suffolk/Nassau/Hempstead PD; Thomas D. Homan (Deputy Director) credited the operation with a decrease in MS-13-related violent crime → https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/joint-operation-nets-24-transnational-gang-members-475-total-arrests-under-operation (Mar. 29, 2018)
- ~60 of 400+ arrested in the Long Island operation came as unaccompanied minors; ORR memo Aug. 16, 2017 — minors with alleged gang affiliation held in highly restrictive facilities; federal judge in California (Nov. 2017): government "has violated their due process rights"; hearings required → https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/frontline/article/in-crackdown-on-ms-13-a-new-detention-policy-raises-alarms (Feb. 18, 2018)
- NPR/WNYC (Sarah Gonzalez), Dec. 5, 2017: at least 32 immigrant teens on Long Island (crossed without a parent) arrested for alleged gang membership; 14 released by judges as of that Monday; 16-year-old held 5 months, partly because he wrote "503" in a notebook; judge: evidence "many layers of hearsay"; William Freeman (ACLU of N. Cal.): "Arrests were based on nothing more than a third-hand report from an unidentified local police officer"; Timothy Sini (Suffolk PD commissioner, to News 12): "We stand by every single detention that we collaborated with ICE on." → https://www.wgbh.org/news/2017-12-05/teens-arrested-on-gang-suspicion-are-released-due-to-lack-of-evidence
