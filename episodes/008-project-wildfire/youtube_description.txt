In six weeks in early 2015, ICE's Homeland Security Investigations and 215 partner agencies arrested 976 gang members and associates in 282 U.S. cities. It was called Project Wildfire. This case file follows ICE's national gang surges, from Project Razor's Edge in 2013 to the record 1,378-arrest surge of 2017 and Operation Matador on Long Island. It looks at who was arrested, what was seized, how "confirmed gang member" was defined, and why some Long Island detentions did not hold up in court.
