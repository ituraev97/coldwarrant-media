Before dawn on April 28, 2011, more than 1,300 officers from over a dozen agencies hit San Pedro, the harbor district of Los Angeles. Their target: the Rancho San Pedro gang. This case file follows Operation Pirate Town, the undercover gun buys behind it, the "safety zone" injunction, the Mexican Mafia "tax" that prosecutors say flows from the street to a prison cell, and the federal cases that came back in 2019 and 2025.
