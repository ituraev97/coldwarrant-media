## Cold open / III. Pirate Town (April 28, 2011)
- Thursday takedown; >1,300 federal and local officers; Los Angeles harbor area; 80 arrested (66 state weapons/narcotics, 14 federal indictments); ~230 members/associates named in federal and state court documents; nearly three-year investigation; more than a dozen agencies; 12 federal indictments, 26 defendants (meth distribution, illegal weapon sales, immigration offenses); one indictment: three defendants, incl. sale of >1 lb meth; lead defendant allegedly brokered sale of 22 firearms incl. assault rifles; about half of federal defendants face potential life; ~145 state defendants (LADA); 90 firearms bought by informants/undercover; 14 more firearms incl. a silencer seized; gang injunction + nuisance abatement against five locations incl. a pizza parlor, first time filed simultaneously with a major SoCal gang takedown; RSP origins 1970s, 600 documented members, 400+ associates; quotes: Birotte "staggering blow", Arnold, Torres (short-barreled shotguns, machine guns, assault-style weapons, "suspected to be linked") → https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20121016194418/http://www.ice.gov/news/releases/1104/110428losangeles.htm
- Operation name "Pirate Town"; 2.5-year investigation that began as a joint ATF/LAPD project to reduce violent crime in San Pedro; agencies ATF, DEA, LAPD, ICE HSI, IRS-CI, U.S. Secret Service, HUD-OIG, CDCR Special Service Unit; drug buys ~1.5 kg cocaine, ~2 kg crack, ~2.5 kg meth, ~2 kg heroin, ~26 lbs marijuana; one primary ATF target at large → https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2011/04/29/operation-pirate-town-targets-over-200-violent-criminals-linked-rancho
- 90+ search warrants; "largest gang take-down in Los Angeles history" (authorities); Beck quote "This is not the first and most certainly won't be the last."; Birotte quote "Our goal is simply this: to dismantle these gangs from top to bottom." → https://cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/san-pedro-sting-leads-to-arrest-of-more-than-100-gang-members
- Predawn hours; ~1,300 officers → https://www.officer.com/investigations/news/10259556/dozens-arrested-after-massive-la-gang-sweep
- Thursday April 28, 2011; Trutanich grew up in San Pedro, quote "I've watched these guys grow from a nothing gang, when I was in high school", "became a cancer"; allegations: witness intimidation, targeting African-Americans, threatening housing project employees; Arnold: children sometimes present; teenage girl with no record allegedly persuaded by her gang-member mother to transport meth; Birotte: earlier sweeps vs 38th Street, Lennox 13, Armenian Power, leaders "thought ... they were untouchable" → https://laist.com/news/kpcc-archive/lapd-and-federal-agents-arrest-80-alleged-members
- Arnold quote "This operation has dealt a major blow to one of the largest, most dangerous and longest-standing street gangs" → https://www.foxnews.com/politics/ice-sweep-nets-80-alleged-gang-members-in-la-area.amp

## V. The tax
- Mexican Mafia founded 1957 at Deuel Vocational Institution, Tracy, Calif.; taxation of drug dealers (Police Magazine, Richard Valdemar, 2007) → https://www.policemag.com/372989/history-of-the-mexican-mafia-prison-gang
- "Secretaries" — intermediaries with ordinary jobs who visit imprisoned members, hold tax money, relay messages (other L.A. case) → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/two-mexican-mafia-secretaries-sentenced-federal-prison-terms-being-intermediaries
- June 26, 2019: 14 charged (two indictments); RSP allegedly under Mexican Mafia control; drugs sold from a hotel and two bars on Pacific Avenue; meth, heroin, cocaine, opioids; "taxes" sent to three Mexican Mafia members serving lengthy state sentences for murder; intercepted communications link members to murders, attacks on rivals, discipline; ~45 firearms; 4 arrested, 7 in state custody, 3 fugitives, 10 more on local charges; LAPD + DEA, ATF support; presumption of innocence → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/federal-indictments-charge-14-members-san-pedro-based-street-gang-who-trafficked
- Messersmith: oversaw day-to-day operations, reported to incarcerated Mexican Mafia members; Aug 2017–May 2018 sold dozens of oxycodone pills, arranged heroin/meth sales, planned smuggling drugs into a prison; May 2018 recorded call "That's a touchdown like Peyton Manning."; pleaded guilty Sept 2020; sentenced Jan 14, 2021 to 120 months by Judge Stephen V. Wilson → https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/rancho-san-pedro-gang-leader-sentenced-10-years-federal-prison-role-heroin-oxycodone
