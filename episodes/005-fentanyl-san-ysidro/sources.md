# CASE FILE 005 — sources (claim → page opened)

## Cold open / IV. The couriers — Flavio Diego Rivera Davalos (Dec. 8, 2017)
- Dec. 8, 2017 arrest at San Ysidro; 2010 Ford Focus, Baja California plates; driver, sole occupant, registered owner; age 19, Tijuana resident, visa holder; CBP officer + canine on pre-primary roving operations alerted at passenger rear door area and rear seats; said he was going to "Las Americas" Premium Outlets; secondary: 31 packages in driver/passenger quarter panels, rear seats, speaker box, firewall under the hood; ~77.82 lbs fentanyl; SDCA ports FY2015 six seizures 30.18 kg vs FY2017 542.42 kg (914% increase) → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/defendant-charged-smuggling-77-pounds-deadly-fentanyl-united-states-one-largest
- Sentencing (release Aug. 6, 2018): 87 months; defense asked for 30 months; Judge Larry A. Burns: did not play a "minor" role; troubling that a college student who was also a teacher failed to set a good example for his young students; expert opinion in court records: 800,000 potentially fatal doses, >$2M; traffickers gave him the car and money to put it in his name, told him to "burn" the plates by crossing repeatedly; gave car to traffickers the day before so it could be loaded; three-vehicle caravan, suspected all three loaded; guilty plea → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/20-year-old-tijuana-college-student-sentenced-87-months-custody-smuggling-fentanyl

## I. The busiest border
- "Busiest Land Port of Entry in Western Hemisphere"; 70,000 northbound vehicles and 20,000 northbound pedestrians per day; $741 million expansion, phases completed 2011–2019 → https://gsa.gov/SanYsidro
- Anne Maricich (acting Director of Field Operations, CBP San Diego): officers sift through ~150,000 northbound travelers daily; traffickers use people, including teens, to avoid suspicion → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/fentanyl-seizures-border-continue-spike-making-san-diego-national-epicenter-fentanyl

## II. The product
- 2 mg considered lethal; 1 gram (sugar packet) could kill up to 500 people; DEA lab testing 5 of 10 pills (2024) vs 7 of 10 (2023), 6 of 10 (2022) → https://www.dea.gov/press-releases/2024/12/16/overdose-deaths-decline-fentanyl-threat-looms
- Sinaloa Cartel and CJNG dominant fentanyl suppliers; precursors primarily from China and India; ~5 of 10 fake pills contain 2 mg+; fentanyl purity declined throughout 2024 (precursor access); DEA seized 61.1 million fake pills in 2024 (−24%) → DEA 2025 National Drug Threat Assessment: https://www.campusdrugprevention.gov/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025-National-Drug-Threat-Assessment.pdf
- Counterfeit M30 / oxycodone look-alikes; Braverman quote "Counterfeit pills are especially dangerous because users often don't know they are ingesting fentanyl" → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/defendant-pleads-guilty-importing-over-20000-fentanyl-pills-san-ysidro-port-entry
- JFK: 14 pill press die sets from five shipments from China (Operation Artemis, Sept. 2023) → DVIDS 900941 description https://www.dvidshub.net/video/900941

## III. The hide
- DHS: over 90% of fentanyl trafficked in cars and trucks at ports of entry; Blue Lotus launched March 13, 2023; first month >4,000 lbs fentanyl at POEs, 156 arrests, >3,500 lbs meth; canine units, non-intrusive inspection → https://dhs.gov/news/2023/04/14/operation-blue-lotus-stops-over-4000-pounds-fentanyl-first-month
- ~87% of fentanyl seized Jan–Aug 2026 at official ports of entry; ~7,000 lbs Jan–Aug 2026, ~1% more than same period 2025 (CBP data) → https://usafacts.org/answers/how-much-fentanyl-is-seized-at-us-borders/country/united-states/
- June 2023 San Ysidro: 61 packages of colored fentanyl pills, 42.15 lbs, $2.25M → DVIDS 893769 https://www.dvidshub.net/video/893769
- Officers test substance to identify drug → DVIDS 984484 https://www.dvidshub.net/video/984484
- CBP LSS Forward Operating Lab at San Ysidro (March 2021) → DVIDS 811874 https://www.dvidshub.net/video/811874
- Seized property vault: drugs held until prosecution complete, then incinerated → DVIDS 994254 https://www.dvidshub.net/video/994254
- Hiding places list: quarter panels, rear seats, speaker box, firewall (Davalos, above); spare tire (2016 case, below); roof (April 2026, below)

## IV. The couriers
- FY2025: 84% U.S. citizens; 83% male; average age 35; average sentence 79 months → https://www.ussc.gov/research/quick-facts/fentanyl-trafficking
- Fernando Jesus Peraza: Aug. 8, 2018, ~2:30 a.m., San Ysidro; ~20,000 counterfeit fentanyl pills made to look like M30/oxycodone in passenger-side rear quarter panel; Braverman quote "This is the biggest fentanyl-pill seizure we've seen along the Southwest Border, and it's likely a national record." → https://timesofsandiego.com/crime/2018/08/08/feds-make-another-record-fentanyl-seizure-in-san-ysidro/
- Peraza: U.S. citizen living in Tijuana, worked for a trash collection service in the U.S.; pleaded guilty; 10-year mandatory minimum → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/defendant-pleads-guilty-importing-over-20000-fentanyl-pills-san-ysidro-port-entry (not named in the video)

## V. The epicenter
- FY2022 first 9 months: San Diego + Imperial counties 5,091 lbs ≈ 60% of 8,425 lbs nationwide; San Diego Sector Border Patrol first tracked fentanyl in 2016 (71 lbs); San Diego County fentanyl deaths 33 (2016) → at least 817 (2021), +2,375%; "YOUR FENTANYL KILLS" poster at San Ysidro and Otay Mesa; Grossman quote "A decade ago, we didn't even know about fentanyl, and now it's a national crisis."; distribution resulting in death: 20-year mandatory minimum → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/fentanyl-seizures-border-continue-spike-making-san-diego-national-epicenter-fentanyl
- Blue Lotus (Mar. 13–May 10, 2023) S.D./C.D. Cal.: ~4,721 lbs fentanyl, 1,700 lbs precursors, 200+ arrests; +85 HSI agents, +35 CBP officers; one vehicle: 116 packages, ~163 lbs; Grossman quote "We are an epicenter for fentanyl trafficking into the United States."; FY2022: 317 fentanyl defendants, +1,800% vs FY2017 → https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdca/pr/fentanyl-enforcement-surge-results-massive-increase-seizures-and-arrests
