On December 8, 2017, a dog at the San Ysidro Port of Entry alerted on a Ford Focus. Inside its panels, seats and speaker box: about 77 pounds of fentanyl. This case file follows how fentanyl crosses the busiest land border in the Western Hemisphere — the cartels and chemicals behind it, the couriers in the driver's seat, the dogs, scanners and secondary inspections that catch it, the 2023 Blue Lotus surge, and what the courts did next.
