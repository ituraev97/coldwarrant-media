Convicted twice, a Mexican Mafia leader still gave orders from a federal prison cell — the true story of Peter "Sana" Ojeda, the 2016 Santa Ana RICO trial, and the Orange County cases that followed through October 2026. Mexican Mafia, La Eme, Operation Black Flag, prison gang history.

On Oct. 30, 2026, federal prosecutors charged three men tied to the Orange County Mexican Mafia with murder in aid of racketeering — every one of them is presumed innocent. This video goes back to the man at the center of the 2011 Operation Black Flag takedown: Peter Ojeda, 73, found guilty by a federal jury on Jan. 13, 2016 and sentenced to 15 years on May 9, 2016. He died in federal custody on June 7, 2018. Everything said here about the 2018, 2022 and 2026 cases is an allegation unless a conviction is named.
