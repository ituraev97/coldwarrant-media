## B. Ojeda — court record and DOJ
5. USAO C.D. Cal., Release 16-005, Jan. 13/14, 2016 (verdict) — https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/mexican-mafia-member-who-oversaw-latino-street-gangs-orange-county-decades-found-guilty
   - Ojeda, 73, "Sana," "The Big Homie"; guilty of RICO conspiracy and conspiracy to commit violent crimes in aid of racketeering (VICAR); conspiracies involved plots to murder rival gangsters in a turf war with another Mexican Mafia member; he helped run OC Mexican Mafia (murder, extortion, drug trafficking); required street gangs to pay a share of proceeds; Suzie Rodriguez, 53, "eyes, ears, and voice"; prior 2005 RICO case, pleaded guilty, sentenced late 2006 to 14 years; Operation Black Flag charged 99 defendants in 2011; 59 federal convictions, OC DA prosecuted the other 40; U.S. Attorney Eileen Decker: "Today's verdict demonstrates our ongoing commitment to using all available tools to dismantle the Mexican Mafia."
6. USAO C.D. Cal., Release 16-102, May 9, 2016 (sentencing) — https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/mexican-mafia-member-who-controlled-latino-street-gangs-orange-county-sentenced-15
   - 15 years, Judge James V. Selna; "danger to the community"; trial roughly 2.5 months; Operation Black Flag by the Santa Ana Gang Task Force; Rodriguez sentencing June 6.
7. USAO C.D. Cal., Release 05-093, June 15, 2005 (first case) — https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/cac/Pressroom/pr2005/093.html
